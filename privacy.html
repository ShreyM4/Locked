PRIVACY NOTICE

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This privacy notice for Browser Lock ("we", "us", or "our") describes how and why we might collect, store, use, and/or process your information when you use our Chrome Extension.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

In Short: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or browsing data.

Browser Lock operates 100% locally and offline in your browser:

  • No Browsing History: We do not collect or monitor your visited websites, URLs, search queries, or page content.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, or device identifiers.
  • No Remote Communication: The extension contains no tracking code, telemetry, analytics, or external server connections.

2. HOW IS YOUR DATA STORED?

All data created by the extension is stored strictly on your local machine using Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local):

  • PIN Data: Your PIN is never stored in plaintext. It is cryptographically salted and hashed using PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations) directly on your device.
  • 2FA Secret Key: The TOTP recovery key is generated randomly on your device and validated locally according to standard RFC 6238 protocols.

3. WHAT PERMISSIONS DO WE USE AND WHY?

The extension requires the following browser permissions solely to provide its lock screen functionality:

  • storage: Used to save your encrypted PIN hash, recovery secret, and preferences on your device.
  • alarms: Used to handle lockout delay timers and auto-lock timers.
  • identity & identity.email: Used strictly to identify your signed-in Google account email locally so that your Google Authenticator app displays an identifiable profile label. This information is never transmitted anywhere.
  • idle (optional): Used only if you choose to enable the inactivity auto-lock feature in Settings.

4. THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to outside parties. No data leaves your local computer sandbox.

5. OPEN SOURCE & VERIFICATION

Browser Lock is an open-source project. The source code is publicly available on GitHub for independent security and privacy audits.

6. CONTACT US

If you have questions or comments about this notice, you may contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
